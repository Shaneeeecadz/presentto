As-Is Business Process (Phase 1: Acquisition)

Purpose: To detail the initial stages of the client engagement lifecycle.
  • 1. Client Inquiry and Assessment: Prospective institution contacts Enrollex; needs are assessed against standard features (student/course management, tracking, basic reports).
  • 2. Scope Evaluation: Out-of-box requests are evaluated. Complex features result in rejection, standard alternatives, or a custom quotation.
  • 3. Agreement and Billing: Scope is accepted, agreement finalized, and payment terms executed.

As-Is Business Process (Phase 2: Deployment)

Purpose: To detail the execution, training, and maintenance phases of the process.

Business Rules and Policies

Purpose: To outline the restrictive guidelines Enrollex uses to standardize service delivery.
  • Standardization Rule: Provides a ready-made template; will NOT develop a new system from scratch for a single client.
  • Boundary Enforcement: Complex third-party integrations are not automatically included and evaluated separately.
  • Configuration Limits: Only supported settings (e.g., institution name) are included without extra charges.
  • Support Scope: Strictly limited to keeping the standard system functional, secure, and usable.

Problem Identification & Impacts

Purpose: To present the three primary bottlenecks currently hindering Enrollex Technologies.
  • 1. Manual Evaluation of Custom Requests: Causes severe pre-sales bottlenecks, delays revenue, and requires heavy admin effort.
  • 2. Misalignment of Client Expectations: Clients assuming custom workflows are free leads to dissatisfaction and contract cancellations.
  • 3. Resource Strain During Orientation: Manual, consultant-led training limits simultaneous onboarding and restricts scalability.
Source: PDF Pages 5-6
Slide 12 / 14

Business Improvement Opportunities

Purpose: To present proposed solutions that directly address the identified operational problems.
  • Streamline Client Qualification: Implement automated self-assessment checklists prior to sales consultations.
  • Standardize Customization Pricing: Develop a predefined, tiered pricing matrix for frequent configurations to reduce negotiation.
  • Automate Client Onboarding: Transition to a self-paced, digital onboarding academy with video guides for simultaneous training.

Summary and Conclusions

Purpose: To conclude the presentation by tying together the process analysis.
  • Operational Baseline: Enrollex operates on a strict service-provider model delivering standardized software.
  • Process Bottlenecks: Manual evaluations, misaligned expectations, and resource-heavy manual training severely limit scalability.
  • Strategic Path Forward: Automate client qualification, standardize customization pricing, and digitize the administrator orientation process.
